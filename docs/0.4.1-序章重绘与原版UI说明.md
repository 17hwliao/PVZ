# 0.4.1 序章重绘与原版UI复用

本轮依据对0.4.0的具体视觉反馈重做序章与UI。游戏机制和数值继续沿用0.4.0，未擅自修改关卡平衡。

## 漫画成品

实际工具为Codex内置imagegen；使用已有act1漫画作为Q版笔触参考，以首组新图固定僵王、制服和囚室身份，再生成电刑、越狱的同风格连续镜头。没有声称通过Clip Studio Paint或Krita手绘。

三张图集均为2×2彩色完整绘制分镜，最终保存为game/prologue/prison-a.png、shock-b.png、escape-c.png，共12格。运行时裁取对应分镜，20段字幕共163秒。移除了prison-renderer.js和囚室Canvas，图片读取失败显示完整文字与继续入口，不回退几何拼图。

- 囚室：全景囚禁、扭身抓紧铁链、凶狠坚果长老与戴夫施压、戴夫按下开关。角色穿着、姿态和表情完整绘制。
- 电刑：手指张开、咬牙闭眼流泪、弓背挣扎、低头脱力。电极位于头部和胸口，植物守卫表情严厉。没有血腥伤口。
- 幻想营救：挣脱束缚踉跄起身、双手挥杆、同伴搀扶、沿通道半扶半跑。保留蓝色电流伏笔，不在玩家字幕中提前揭晓幻想。

这些是绘制好的不同动作关键姿势，搭配推镜、震动和转场，不是持续逐帧的角色动画或配音视频。柔和模式减少闪白和晃动；开头11段现实，越狱起最后9段幻想的叙事边界保持。

## 专业工具查询与采用方式

查询了[Clip Studio Paint漫画工作流](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/comics-manga/)、[分格与漫画文档](https://help.clip-studio.com/en-us/manual_en/540_comic/Creating_comics_and_webtoons.htm)及[Krita绘画和动画功能](https://krita.org/en/features/)。分格、角色连续性和外置字幕组织参考了这些专业工具支持的制作方法；本轮实际图片生成仍为内置imagegen，没有伪称使用这些桌面软件。

## 最终制作提示词集

以下为最终制作规格与三组提示词归档；模型生成存在随机性，重新生成不保证像素一致。原始成品图集已直接随源码保存。

### 通用规格

Create a production-ready hand-painted chibi anime comic atlas for the Plants vs Zombies fan story. Exactly FOUR equal landscape panels in a strict 2 by 2 grid, overall 16:9. Match the supplied act1 comic reference: expressive ink lines, painted textured shadows, cinematic warm/cold lighting, detailed environment, fully drawn characters and hands. George Edgar has a huge bald grey-green head, white lab coat, red tie, dark trousers and boots; keep identity and costume consistent. Draw whole character acting and physical contact, not pasted game sprites or simple geometric props. Plants and human interrogators look hostile and intimidating, zombies look vulnerable and civilized. No words, no speech balloons, no logos; allow room for external subtitles. Non-graphic, no gore, no human eating.

### A · prison-a

Four frames of a damp stone interrogation cell. Top left: wide view of George chained to a heavy electric chair, shoulders hunched and arms restrained, cables and chest/head electrodes; armed plant guards and a harsh observation window. Top right: George twists against the restraints, gripping chains, worried exhausted face, visible strained fingers and body. Bottom left: stern angry walnut elder and menacing human Dave behind the observation window, assertive pointing and commanding gestures, guard spears and uniforms. Bottom right: Dave presses the electric switch firmly, his expression cold and threatening; restrained George is visible through glass. Maintain cell geography and visual continuity. Fully illustrated painted scenes matching reference, not vector blocks.

### B · shock-b

Use the supplied new prison atlas as the character and room continuity reference. Four distinct electroshock acting frames in the same cell. Top left: first shock, fingers splayed and shoulders forced back, chains taut and blue current at electrodes. Top right: close view with eyes squeezed shut, teeth clenched, tears and sweat, hands locked on restraints; hostile walnut guard behind. Bottom left: George arches back and cries out, head tilted, whole body and arms straining against the chair, powerful but non-graphic blue electric light. Bottom right: spent and slumped forward, shoulders dropped, hands limp, still fully chained; cruel guards remain at their posts. Preserve white coat, red tie, large grey-green head and facial identity. Clear pain and different poses in every panel, no exposed wounds or gore.

### C · escape-c

Use shock atlas for identity and old escape reference only for staging. Four complete comic scenes following electric torture. Top left: George staggers upright beside apparently broken chair restraints, bent knees and shaking hands; faint blue static at edge. Top right: he swings a metal rod with BOTH hands and a clear body turn at a walnut guard, non-graphic action, guard recoils. Bottom left: ordinary zombie and bucket veteran enter and support his weight with hands under his arms and shoulder, exhausted George leans on them. Bottom right: the companions half-carry and half-run with him through a dark corridor, boots moving and coats trailing; hostile plant silhouettes pursue behind. Keep the same character and costume, credible contact, detailed hand-painted room, warm refuge light, tiny electric edge noise, no explicit dream reveal.
